Install and open the app (2 minutes, once)

The app isn’t sold through the App Store, so the first time you open it macOS shows a warning and blocks it. That’s normal for independent Mac apps. Here’s exactly how to get past it.

Download for Mac Free, pay what you want. You get a .dmg file to open in step 1.

Install in 5 steps

  1. Drag the app into Applications

    Open the downloaded SwitchAccountForClaude.dmg and drag the app onto the Applications folder.

    →Applications

  2. Open it once, then click Done

    Open the app from Applications. macOS shows “Switch Account for Claude” Not Opened and mentions malware. That only means Apple hasn’t reviewed it. Click Done. Don’t click “Move to Bin”, which deletes the app.

    ⚠️
    “Switch Account for Claude” Not Opened Apple could not verify “Switch Account for Claude” is free of malware that may harm your Mac or compromise your privacy.
    DoneMove to Bin

  3. Go straight to Privacy & Security and scroll to the bottom

    Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and scroll all the way down, past all the privacy permissions, to the Security section. There you’ll find “Switch Account for Claude” was blocked to protect your Mac. Click Open Anyway.

  4. Confirm with your password, then Open Anyway

    Enter your Mac password or use Touch ID. One more window appears, and this time it has an Open Anyway button. Click it. macOS won’t ask again.

  5. Allow it to control Claude

    On the first switch, macOS asks whether the app may control Claude. Click Allow: the app needs it to quit Claude before swapping accounts. Then sign in to your second account once, and you’re set.

Shortcut for developers

Prefer Terminal? Skip steps 2–4 with one command

It removes the “downloaded from the internet” flag from this one app, so macOS lets it open. Run it after step 1, then open the app normally and continue with step 5.

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Switch Account for Claude.app"

Copied. Paste it into Terminal (Applications → Utilities → Terminal) and press Return.

Why does macOS warn about this app?

Apple shows this for every app that isn’t notarized through its paid developer program, whatever the app does. Switch Account for Claude only moves Claude’s own folders on your Mac. Your chats and logins never leave your computer; the app only checks marcobestonzo.com for updates.

If something doesn’t match

I don’t see “Open Anyway” in Privacy & Security

macOS only shows the button for about an hour after a blocked attempt. Open the app again, click Done, then go straight back to Privacy & Security and scroll to the very bottom. Or use the Terminal shortcut further up this page.

It says the app “is damaged and can’t be opened”

The download was changed on the way, usually by unzipping it with another tool. Download it again and open the .dmg by double-clicking it in Finder.

I accidentally clicked “Move to Bin”

Open the Bin, drag the app back into Applications, and start again from step 2.

Which Macs does it work on?

macOS 12 Monterey or later, on Apple Silicon and Intel. You need the Claude desktop app and two Claude accounts.
